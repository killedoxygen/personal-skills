---
name: security-campaign
description: Sustain authorized vulnerability research across sessions; use for ongoing campaigns, not one-off scans or reviews.
---
Follow the strongest leads; choose experiments that could disprove your hypothesis.
Establish attacker reachability and security impact before calling something a vulnerability.
Preserve reproducible evidence and source revisions; log findings, failed leads, open questions, and the next experiment.
Clean up one-off resources and unneeded artifacts, VMs, and containers after preserving necessary evidence, a cleanup log, and a recipe to rebuild them.
Change direction when evidence stalls; use delegation and coordination only when they help.
